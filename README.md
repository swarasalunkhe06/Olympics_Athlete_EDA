# 🏅 Olympic Athlete Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of historical Olympic athlete data to uncover patterns and trends in athlete participation, demographics, sports, and medal achievements.

Using Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn, the analysis explores Olympic history and identifies meaningful insights through data cleaning, statistical analysis, and visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the age distribution of Olympic athletes.
- Explore male and female participation across Olympic editions.
- Compare Summer and Winter Olympic participation trends.
- Identify the most popular sports and events.
- Analyze medal distribution and country-wise performance.
- Explore medal achievements across different age groups.
- Understand country strengths across different sports.

## 📊 Key Analysis & Visualizations

1. Athlete Age Distribution
2. Participation by Gender

4. Top 15 Sports by Participation

13. Country-wise Medal Performance by Sport

## 🧹 Data Preprocessing

- Checked dataset structure and data types.
- Identified missing values.
- Detected and removed exact duplicate records.
- Handled missing medal values by assigning "No Medal".
- Examined missing values in Age, Height, and Weight.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Most Olympic athlete-event records are concentrated between the ages of 20 and 30.
- Male athletes historically account for a larger share of participation.
- Female participation has increased across Olympic editions.
- Athletics, Gymnastics, and Swimming are among the most represented sports.
- The United States leads in historical gold-medal athlete-event records.
- Medal achievements vary across age groups, countries, and sports.
